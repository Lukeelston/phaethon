# Eridanus race audio

| Cue | Recording | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rival engine | Kinoton, [Car, BMW M6, Start, Idle, Accelerate](https://freesound.org/s/478597/) | Mono 48 kHz excerpt starting at 3.68 seconds. The 1.45-second loop has a 0.20-second crossfade and a 70 Hz high-pass filter. Each craft carries its source. |
