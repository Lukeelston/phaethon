# Engine loops

Baked by `tools/engine_bake.py` from the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** (Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via the FreePats SFZ+FLAC edition. Every engine sound here is resampled, granulated and filtered piano: hammer strikes become combustion pulses, sustains become turbine whine, damper noise becomes roar.
