Experimental Desktop Racer - Windows x64 - v0.2.5

Two engines. One cockpit. We know it's a little fucky.

The Network now has fixed geographic landscapes: grassy plains and farms,
tundra, rocky ranges, desert, snowy mountains, wetlands, jungle and urban
districts. Regional plants, settlements, water and clouds give the highways
a sense of place. Mountains remain fixed in the world and clear the roads.
Jump approaches now climb in one continuous bow, with varied heights and
smooth recovery descents. Service courts, solar fields, wind turbines and
basalt spires fill out the countryside. Distant roads reach into the haze,
terrain extends farther, and new scenery fades into view.

Skill points reward sustained speed, clean fast turns, close calls and settled
jump landings. Chain clean maneuvers for up to a 5x multiplier. Impacts break
the chain but keep earned points; restart begins a new run. Best scores save
locally for each course. Traffic yields when a safe lane is available. Enable
Driving partner for formation flying and occasional passes on wide highways.
The total rests quietly at upper right, moves to top center while points are
earned, then fades back to its corner. The partner yields around your full craft.

GET IN
1. Extract the whole ZIP into a folder.
2. Keep Phaethon.exe and Phaethon.pck together.
3. Double-click Phaethon.exe. Choose the 100-mile sprint first.
No Godot editor, account, headset, or online connection is required to play.

WHAT YOU BOUGHT
An offline, single-player physics experiment with five destinations:
- 100-mile sprint: build speed, survive, then stop in the finish box.
- Grand Prix: a circuit with tighter Starfighter handling.
- Eridanus: a long course to explore and push your luck on.
- Emergency Run: an experimental delivery mission on Eridanus.
- The Network: experimental linked roads and cities to explore.
You steer using the difference between your left and right engine thrust.
This is a small experimental build, not a finished campaign. Expect strange
handling, sparse scenery, rough collisions, and the occasional spectacular
mistake. There is no multiplayer. This download is the flat-screen edition.
Future features, updates, and other platforms are not part of this purchase.

The Network has twenty ascent ramps: staged climbs, longer gaps, four jumps
that transfer onto another highway, and four twisting recovery decks.
Minimum launch speeds vary from 2,000 to 4,000 mph. Signs and the approach
display show the speed needed at the launch lip. Keep throttle through the climb.
North/south expressways rise above the lower east/west roads. The lower level
has more slow traffic; the upper level has faster commuters and more hot rods.
Traffic changes lanes for faster vehicles when there is time and room.
Each region has its own landmarks, and traffic has five distinct silhouettes.
Signs are readable from both sides; reverse faces identify the other approach.
Twelve side roads add sweeping corners; vertical shafts turn you around at
the highway ends. Driving partner adds a teal craft that follows your speed.
Toggle it in the main menu or Escape pause menu; your choice is saved.
The main-menu Obstacle collisions checkbox controls bridge pillars and
traffic. It defaults off and saves your choice. Guardrails always keep you
on the road; ground contact and jump landings remain active.

FIRST FLIGHT
Hold E briefly to raise both throttles, then release it. Throttle stays where
you leave it. Hold D to reduce both; X cuts both. Engine thrust does not brake
you: hold A and Right Arrow together for both air brakes. Brake early.
Use unequal thrust or individual air brakes to turn. Small inputs are useful.
In Speedcraft, air brakes gain drag and turning grip above 1,250 mph. Brake
on the side you want to turn toward; both together slow you in a straight line.
Backspace returns you to the track but keeps the throttle setting. Cut with X
first, or use Escape > Restart course for a completely fresh start.

KEYBOARD
E / D              Both throttles up / down
W / S              Left throttle up / down
Up / Down          Right throttle up / down
A / Right          Left / right air brake (hold both to brake straight)
X                  Cut both throttles
Space              Reignite engines
H                  Hold to repair
R                  Arm ramjet
M                  Switch handling mode
Backspace          Reset to track (keeps throttle)
Escape             Pause / resume; restart, menu and quit are available here

CONTROLLERS
Keyboard is the supported starting point. Gamepad support is experimental:
left and right stick forward control the matching engines; triggers brake.
A reignites, X repairs, Y arms the ramjet, Back resets. Keyboard controls take
priority until you return the sticks/triggers to neutral. Physical controller
compatibility has not been verified for this build. Printed lever hardware is
not included and is not required.

SYSTEM AND KNOWN LIMITS
Windows x64 desktop with a Vulkan-capable graphics driver. The release was
render-tested on Windows with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080 at 1280 x 720.
Minimum GPU and performance on other machines have not been established.
Mac, Linux, mobile, standalone Quest and VR are not supported by this package.
The executable is unsigned. Download it from the ElstonWorks product page.
The window can be resized; Escape provides controls if you get lost.

Local game data is stored under the Windows application-data directory:
ElstonWorks/Phaethon. The game has no online account or telemetry integration.
