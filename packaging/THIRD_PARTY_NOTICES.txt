Engine audio - Salamander Grand Piano
------------------------------------
The 24 engine-loop and one-shot WAV files in audio/engines are adaptations of
Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5), recorded by Alexander Holm, from the
FreePats SFZ+FLAC V3+20200602 edition assembled by Roberto for FreePats.

Original recordings: Alexander Holm.
Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Full license: licenses/CC-BY-3.0.txt
Source package's original attribution: licenses/SALAMANDER_SOURCE_README.txt

Changes for Phaethon: tools/engine_bake.py resamples, granulates, filters,
layers, saturates and loops the piano recordings. Hammer strikes become
combustion pulses and pops; sustained notes become turbine and rotor tones;
damper releases become noise and crackle. The adaptations include band-limited
copies for pitch shifting. They are engine sound effects, not the original
playable piano instrument. No endorsement by the original authors is implied.

These adapted audio files are provided under CC BY 3.0. Any general terms for
the game do not restrict the rights granted by that license for this material.
The original piano sample library and the Python audio-processing tools used
to make these effects are not included in the player download.

The wind, traffic and course effects are synthesized by the game's scripts.
