Two engines. One cockpit. We know it’s a little fucky.

Phaethon is a scrappy, offline Windows racing and flight experiment. Balance two engines, wrestle with absurd speed, and discover how far ahead you should have started braking.

$3 includes both editions: a keyboard-first desktop game and the seated PC VR cockpit build. Two separate ZIP downloads, with launch instructions, controls and license credits. No Godot editor needed.

  • 100-mile sprint: build speed, survive, and stop in the finish box.
  • Grand Prix: a circuit with tighter Starfighter handling.
  • Eridanus: a long course to explore and push your luck on.

PC VR also keeps the experimental Emergency Run and Network modes. Quest Touch controls provide throttle, brakes, gears, repair, recentering and in-headset course switching. Four control schemes are included. Network means connected roads, not multiplayer.

The honest bit: a small playable prototype with odd handling, rough collisions, unfinished scenery and spectacular mistakes. Offline, single-player. Buy this build for what it is; future features, updates and platforms are not promised.

Requirements: Windows x64 and a Vulkan-capable graphics driver. Desktop was render-tested at 1280 × 720 on an RTX 5080; minimum hardware and performance elsewhere are not established. PC VR needs a gaming PC, configured OpenXR runtime and connected headset/controllers; its layout is designed around Quest Touch over Link or Air Link. This is not a standalone Quest APK. Mac, Linux and mobile are not supported. Both executables are unsigned.

Desktop first flight: hold E briefly, then release. D eases off; X cuts thrust. Hold A and Right Arrow together to brake. Escape opens pause and course selection. For VR, connect the headset’s PC session first, run PhaethonVR.exe, click either stick to recenter, then squeeze the triggers gently.
