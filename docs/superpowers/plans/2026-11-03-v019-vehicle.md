## Post-release single-grip turning investigation

- Luke reports weaker turning with **one grip at a time**, correcting the initial description of weak dual-grip braking. The v019 headset log's zero left-grip readings do not establish a controller fault: they are consistent with using the right grip alone. That run had active OpenXR tracking and full right-grip input reaching the expected high-speed 0.35/0.65 brake mapping.
- Quest input mapping and brake force equations match the accepted v016 handling. Controlled comparisons across v016, v018 and v019 give identical open-space turn angles and speed; focused Godot feathering (20/20) and speed-brake tests pass. This does not establish the cause of the subjective regression.
- Network trials match before wall contact. At K1, sustained full one-hand braking can reach the guardrail and its reaction can reverse some of the nose rotation. This is a reproduced candidate, **not a confirmed explanation of Luke's run**. No physics tuning has been applied.
- Added an opt-in `--brake-trace=ABSOLUTE_PATH` JSONL recorder to the VR cockpit. It observes every physics step for contact counts and minimum grip, samples controllers/input/body dynamics/road position at 10 Hz, flushes once per second, and stops after five active minutes per flight. Ordinary launches leave it disabled. Scene changes append distinct flight records; the final partial sampling interval is not emitted.
- Dedicated player: `build/brake-diagnostic/PhaethonVR.exe`; launch helper: `.tools/launch_brake_diagnostic.ps1`. Existing v019 archives are untouched. This is a diagnostic build, not a handling fix or a new release.

A real Godot K1 contact probe recorded 85 body-contact steps over 65 simulation steps with any contact. Import/export and smoke logs have no script errors.
