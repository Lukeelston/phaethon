# Phaethon: the world

*The world bible. Decided with Luke, 2026-10-19 (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-emergency-run-design.md).
Everything built must agree with it. What's here but not built yet is marked **later**.*

## The planet

- **Twin suns, and the day is lethal.** Life survives in the twilight band, the strip of ground
  between night and day. The dawn line sweeps the ground at about 1,000 mph, roughly STARFIGHTER's
  top speed. In the myth, Phaethon drove the sun's chariot and burned the world. Here you outrun it.
- **Nothing flies.** Above the ground-effect layer the suns cook anything. Repulsor craft, riding a
  metre and a half over the ground, are how everything moves.
- **Information travels by hand.** Communication can't reach past a few miles: the planet's wave
  disruption kills it. And it's wartime, so any shortwave link might be hacked. Dispatch orders,
  intelligence, a hospital's call for help: all of it has to be carried. A fast courier is
  critical infrastructure.
  - **Later:** relay towers, where updates only arrive near one.

## The craft

One airframe, two configurations, switchable on the move: "different types of transport for
different environments."

- **SPEEDCRAFT** is for the open plains, where you build a lead on the dawn. Up to ~4,000 mph on
  the lever, more on the burner.
- **STARFIGHTER** is for the shelters and the towns. Their gates only take you below 1,250 mph,
  and STARFIGHTER is tight enough to fly them.

## Shelter

- **The towns** are shelter. Their gates are the STARFIGHTER ZONE gantries at their mouths.
- **The covered ramps** stand over the open loop's first and last four miles at each town:
  outgoing ramps where you leave a town, receiving ramps where you come in. You reach speed under a
  roof before you're out in the light, and you brake under one coming home. (Luke, 2026-10-19:
  "a 'receiving' and 'outgoing' onramp ... such that the vehicle can reach speed inside of a
  structure before being exposed to light.")
- **Shade lines** are privately owned covered routes. You twist into one through a corkscrew, ride
  the underside of its canopy upside down where the suns can't reach you, and corkscrew back
  upright into the shelter at its end. They're normally closed to you: a gate at the mouth says
  PRIVATE LINE unless your job authorizes you, and like a town's gate it only takes you below
  1,250 mph.

## The jobs

Each kind of delivery sets its own rules.

- **Emergencies** (built: the emergency run): security or medical. They authorize speed (SPEED
  AUTHORIZED) and unlock the private shade lines.
- **Dawn shift** (**later**): miners out before sunrise. The game starts here, in a much slower
  craft, with time and G limits.
- **Evening commercial** (**later**): cargo at dusk, with no time or G limits.
- **Progression** (**later**): finishing jobs earns faster craft and route permits.

## Eridanus

A green river valley ringed by mountains: a 199 km course with two towns (the start town and the
lake town) joined by a SPEEDCRAFT loop.

- **The shade line** leaves the loop's final straight 9 mi out from the start town and runs 10 mi
  under its canopy into the start town's shelter. It's slower than the open loop, and safer.
- **The medical pad** lies on a straight in the lake town.

### The emergency run

A courier relay brings word: the lake town has had a mine collapse and needs a medical team before
dawn.

1. **Authorized.** You're SPEED AUTHORIZED and the shade line opens. The dash shows the job, where,
   and the time until the dawn reaches the lake town.
2. **Out.** The start town, its outgoing ramp, the loop, the lake town's receiving ramp, into the
   lake town.
3. **The drop.** Stop on the pad and hold while the team gets off.
4. **Home, ahead of the dawn.** It comes up over the lake town soon after you leave it, a wall of
   light you can always see, and sweeps across the valley toward the start town. The loop swings
   you round to meet it: on the final straight you're flying toward its light, racing it to cover.
   Get back under cover before it reaches you: the start town's receiving ramp on the open loop, at
   4,000 mph, or the shade line's canopy, slower and upside down.
5. **The result.** Made it, by how many seconds, against your best. Caught in the open, the light
   burns everything white.

The run is flown before dawn, in twilight. Free Eridanus keeps its afternoon.
